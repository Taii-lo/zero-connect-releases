## Vehicle photos

This software does not contain manufacturer photographs. When you pick a model and color,
your own device downloads the matching picture from the manufacturer's public image server
and keeps it in its local data directory. You can instead upload a photo of your own bike;
it never leaves your device. The photographs remain the property of their owners.
